# Accurate connection logos

All fourteen third-party service logos now live in the compiled asset catalog: Gmail, Drive, Google Calendar, Google Chat, Google Tasks, WhatsApp, GitHub, Notion, Slack, Todoist, Linear, Instagram, X and Telegram. Apple Notes and Calendar continue to use native macOS app icons. Files & documents retains a folder symbol because it is a local feature.

The logos no longer depend on whether the corresponding app is installed. Original colors and aspect ratios are preserved. White variants improve GitHub, Linear and X legibility on Iris's dark surface. Connection document rows and details reuse the same artwork at smaller sizes.

New artwork: https://github.com/homarr-labs/dashboard-icons (Apache 2.0 library; brand trademarks remain with their owners). License and source credits are bundled as ConnectionLogoCredits.txt. Existing Gmail, Drive and Calendar artwork is retained.

Validation: visually reviewed the downloaded logos; Debug build passed; all fourteen named images loaded successfully from the built application's asset catalog. No runtime logo downloads are needed.
